How it works

Three steps. The first one only once.

1

Set up once

You send a short phone video of yourself (30 seconds is enough) and confirm your consent. From that we create your avatar and your cloned voice. Once, and never again.

2

Text instead of camera

Bullet points are enough: the script assistant turns them into a spoken script in your tone. Change whatever you like, then click “Create”.

3

Fully produced

Lip-synced, colour-graded, with burned-in subtitles, lower third and logo. Optionally with a new background, and in every format: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9.

Trust

Your face, your voice, your rules.

Synthetic media need clear boundaries. At Persona they are built in, not bolted on.

  • Consent only. Avatars are created exclusively by the person themselves or with their documented consent.
  • Labelling. We recommend and support labelling AI-generated videos as the EU AI Act requires.
  • Hosted in Germany. The platform runs on Hetzner servers. Your data is strictly separated per account.
  • Deletable. Avatar, voice and videos are removed completely on request.
  • Transparency. Voice and lip synthesis run on specialised AI services; which ones is stated in the privacy policy (German).
